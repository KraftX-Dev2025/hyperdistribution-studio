## Description

HyperMixer is the content engine for businesses. Founders, marketing teams and agencies use it to keep every brand posting on every channel, without a content team's budget.

Teach it your brand once. Paste your website and profiles, and HyperMixer writes your brand brain: voice, audience, pillars, hooks, do's and don'ts. Every post after that sounds like you.

Plan the week across 50+ channels. Set a weekly target for Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, X, newsletters, press and more, and the fire grid shows where each brand is behind.

Blast one idea everywhere. Turn a single idea into native posts for every brand and channel at once, each in that brand's voice.

Edit videos from your own media. Pick clips and photos from your library, let HyperMixer cut them into a short-form edit, and save the video straight to your camera roll or share it.

Your business data stays yours. Brands, posts and media stay on your device. AI features use your own Claude API key, which is never stored on our servers.
