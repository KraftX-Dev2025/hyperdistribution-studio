Quick answers

AI features say I need a key. Open Settings and paste a Claude API key from platform.claude.com.

How do I save a finished video? In the app, tap Save or share under the video and choose Save Video or the app you want to post to.

Where is my data? On your device. Use Settings → Export backup to move it to another device. See the privacy policy.
