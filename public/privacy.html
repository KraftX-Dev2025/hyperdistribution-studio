Privacy Policy

HyperMixer · Last updated 20 November 2026

HyperMixer is a content planning and editing tool for businesses. It is built so that your business data stays on your own device. This policy explains what that means.

What stays on your device

Your brands, brand brains, posts, ideas, plans, accounts list, rendered videos and settings are stored only in your browser or in the HyperMixer app on your device. Photos, clips and music you pick are read on your device and are never uploaded by HyperMixer. We do not have accounts, and we cannot see your workspace.

What leaves your device, and why

  • AI requests. When you use an AI feature, the text of that request (and any images you attach to it) is sent through HyperMixer's server to Anthropic, together with the Claude API key you entered. The server passes it on and returns the answer. It does not store your request, the answer or your key. Anthropic processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy, tied to your API key.
  • Reading links. When you ask HyperMixer to learn from a web page, our server fetches that public page and returns its text to you. We do not keep a copy.
  • HeyGen (optional). If you add your HeyGen API key and ask for an avatar video, the script, the avatar you picked and your key are sent through HyperMixer's server to HeyGen, which makes the video under its own terms. The server does not store them.
  • Google Drive (optional). If you connect Google Drive, your browser or app saves copies of your brand brains, brand kits, posts and videos straight into a HyperMixer folder in your own Drive. HyperMixer asks only for access to files it creates, and the sign-in stays on your device.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard technical logs, such as IP address and time of request, for security and reliability, for a limited period.

What we don't do

We do not sell data, show ads, use tracking or analytics SDKs, or build profiles. The apps do not collect data for the developer.

Camera, microphone and photos

The iOS and Android apps ask for access only when you choose to pick or shoot media, or save a finished video. That media stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Delete the app, or clear the site's data in your browser, to remove everything. Export a backup in Settings first if you want to keep a copy.

Children

HyperMixer is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: HyperMixer support.
